# Task: Product Detail Page - Pizza Margherita

Create a dedicated product page for the "Pizza Margherita". This page should feel like a deep dive into the craft behind our signature pizza.

## Page Structure:
1. **Interactive Header**: Consistent with the home page, sticky design.
2. **Product Hero**: High-impact vertical or horizontal layout showing the Margherita pizza in detail.
3. **Craft Details**: A section explaining the San Marzano tomato base, Fior di Latte mozzarella, and the 48-hour dough fermentation.
4. **Order Section**: Price ($12.500), quantity selector, and a premium "Add to Cart" button.
5. **Nutrition & Ingredients**: A toggle-able or clean list of specific ingredients.
6. **Related Delicacies**: A horizontal scroll showing 2-3 other pizzas (Diavola, Quattro Formaggi) to encourage browsing.
7. **Consistent Footer**.

## DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED for Stitch):
* **Visual Theme:** "Heritage-Lux," blending rugged tradition with minimalist digital luxury.
* **Colors:** Troppo Primary Blue (#1E4B99), Midnight Secondary (#0F2A5A), Heritage Gold Accent (#D4AF37).
* **Typography:** High-Weight Playfair Display for headers (black weight, tracking 0.2em), Inter for body and labels.
* **Geometry:** Sharp edges for images and containers (`rounded-none`). Subtly defined by light borders and whisper-soft diffused shadows on hover.
* **Layout:** Generous whitespace between sections to emphasize premium quality.
