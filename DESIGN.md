# Design System: Troppo | Premium Neapolitan Pizza Experience

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The atmosphere is "Heritage-Lux," blending rugged Neapolitan tradition with a clean, modern digital experience. It feels balanced, premium, and airy, utilizing generous whitespace and high-contrast typography to emphasize craft and quality.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Troppo Primary Blue (#1E4B99):** Deep, authoritative blue used for brand identity, primary navigation links, and key CTA borders.
* **Midnight Secondary Blue (#0F2A5A):** Extremely deep blue used for high-density backgrounds (footers, dark overlays) and secondary button states.
* **Heritage Gold Accent (#D4AF37):** A muted sand-gold used for decorative accents, highlight text (e.g., "EST. 2020"), and status indicates.
* **Italian Leaf Green (#008C45):** A vibrant green used for heritage motifs (stripes) and successful interaction states (WhatsApp buttons).
* **Italian Rosso Red (#CD212A):** A classic deep red used for heritage motifs and subtle visual warmth.
* **Background Light (#F9FAFB):** A very light, clean slate-tinted white used for the primary page surface.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Display Font (Playfair Display):** Used for main headings and the brand name. Characterized by High-Weight (Black) styling, often with high letter-spacing (`tracking-[0.2em]`) and uppercase transformation.
* **Sans Font (Inter):** Used for all functional text, menus, and body copy. Highly legible.
* **Menu Labels:** Small caps, bold weights, and wide tracking (`tracking-widest`) to maintain a premium feel.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** 
    - Desktop: Sharp, squared-off edges (`rounded-none`) with thick borders or solid fills.
    - Mobile: Subtly rounded or pill-shaped for ergonomic interaction.
* **Cards/Containers:** Sharp, squared-off edges (`rounded-none`). Subtly defined by extremely light borders (#slate-100) and whisper-soft diffused shadows that only appear on hover.
* **Navigation:** Sticky top bar with glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-md`) and a slight slate border-bottom.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Whitespace:** Generous vertical padding (`py-24`) between major sections to allow imagery to breathe.
* **Grid Alignment:** Centered 7-column max-width (`max-w-7xl`) for desktop layouts.
* **Animations:** Subtle fade-up entrance animations for content, and instant yet soft transitions for hover states.
